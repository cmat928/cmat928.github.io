Tournament Fishing Competitive Intelligence Platform

A solo-founder SaaS product that predicts where fish will be before a tournament starts, then gets smarter automatically after every event — with a privacy architecture designed in from day one, not bolted on after.

Situation

Competitive tournament anglers make high-stakes, high-cost decisions — where to fish, what structure to target — largely on instinct and word of mouth, even though the environmental data that actually drives fish behavior (water temperature, barometric pressure, moon phase, lake levels) is public and free.

Complication

Any product that asks anglers to be GPS-tracked before it's proven predictive value is asking for trust it hasn't earned. And most "gets smarter over time" products make a subtle modeling mistake: treating a poor tournament finish as evidence that "fish weren't there" — when a bad finish is usually about execution, not location, and good anglers actively fish decoy spots to mislead competitors.

Question

Can a prediction product prove its value with zero user effort and zero tracking first — and only ask for opt-in data once trust is established — while still building a model that genuinely improves over time?

Answer

Designed as a phased product, not just a model:

  • Free tier, zero tracking: automated daily pulls from NOAA, USGS, and moon-phase APIs feed an XGBoost model that ships a pre-tournament report — no manual input, no GPS, no cost.
  • Pro tier, opt-in only, introduced later: once free reports have a track record, anglers can opt into passive, anonymized GPS tracking in exchange for higher-resolution predictions.
  • Confidence-weighted correction, not naive inversion: a bad finish is down-weighted toward zero signal rather than treated as proof fish weren't there — and sessions with scattered, low-confidence movement get flagged and excluded rather than trusted.
Privacy architecture, not an afterthought: raw GPS is anonymized at ingestion — before it's queryable by anything else in the system — via 100m geohash clustering with identity stripped. Identified raw tracks live in a separate, encrypted, access-restricted table used only to recompute clustering if the algorithm changes, and are purged after 12 months.

Revenue model: $39/month individual, $149/month for a 6-angler team tier — 130 subscribers hits the $5,000/month milestone.
